## Design Guidelines (All Graphics)

**Colors:**
- Primary: #8B5CF6 (Electric Purple)
- Accent: #06B6D4 (Cyan)
- Background: #0F172A (Deep Black)
- Text: #F1F5F9 (Off White)

**Typography:**
- Headers: Bold, geometric, sans-serif
- Body: Clean, modern (Inter/Helvetica/Arial)
- Avoid script fonts
- High contrast for readability

**Logo Usage:**
- Always on dark backgrounds
- Minimum 10% padding around logo
- Don't distort or stretch
- Use PNG with transparency

**General Style:**
- Professional tech/crypto aesthetic
- Not too corporate, not too casual
- Futuristic but accessible
- High contrast, bold colors
- Minimal but impactful
